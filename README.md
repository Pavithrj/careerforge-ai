# CareerForge AI

An AI-powered career development platform that helps users explore job opportunities, understand their skill gaps, and make informed career decisions.

## Overview

CareerForge AI is a full-stack web application designed to help job seekers manage their career profiles, discover relevant jobs, analyze their suitability for job opportunities, and identify areas for professional growth.

Users will be able to upload a resume or enter their career information manually. The platform will use AI-assisted analysis to provide insights into relevant strengths, potential skill gaps, and job preparation recommendations.

## Planned Features

- **Dashboard:** An overview of job recommendations and career activity.
- **Find Jobs:** Search and filter available job listings or analyze a pasted job description.
- **Resume & Career Profile:** Manage resumes, skills, experience, and career information.
- **AI Career Analysis:** Explore job-match insights, strengths, skill gaps, and preparation recommendations.
- **My Applications:** Track job applications through their different stages. *(Planned for a later phase.)*
- **Career Goals:** Define career goals and track progress. *(Planned for a later phase.)*

## Technology Stack

The planned technology stack includes:

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, and TypeScript
- **Database:** PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM
- **Testing:** Vitest and Playwright
- **Version Control:** Git and GitHub
- **AI Integration:** An LLM API, to be selected during implementation
